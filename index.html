Research Interests
My research interests are primarily motivated by the application area that I am focussing on, e.g. model interpretability for ML applications in medicine. I am interested in machine learning interpretability, graph representation learning, model uncertainty, active learning, and causal inference.

I am passionate about their applications to improve human productivity and decision making in domains like healthcare and finance.
